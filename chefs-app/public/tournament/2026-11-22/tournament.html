CHEFS Club Tournament

A great afternoon of pickleball, friendly competition, and time together. We look forward to seeing you on court.

Total Duration:

2:00 PM - 4:00PM

Tournament Format:

3 Teams · Individual + Team Competition

Teams

RED 🔴 | GREEN 🟢 | BLUE 🔵

Stage 1 – Individual Competition
  • Duration: 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM
  • 4 matches per player, total 36 matches
  • 8 courts, total 4 rounds
  • 11 standard points or 15 minutes, whichever comes first.
  • Groups:
    • A: Mix 2.5–2.75 (8 women, 6 men)
    • B: Women 3.0–3.5 (13 women)
    • C: Men 3.0–3.5 (9 men)
  • Individual points count toward team totals.
Stage 2 – Team Competition
  • Duration: 3:00 PM – 4:00 PM
  • 6 courts · 3 rounds × 15 minutes
  • 11 standard points or 15 minutes, whichever comes first.
  • 12 players per team per matchup
  • Matchups:
    1. 🔴 RED vs 🟢 GREEN
    2. 🔴 RED vs 🔵 BLUE
    3. 🟢 GREEN vs 🔵 BLUE
Scoring & Championship 🏆

Three teams. One championship. Every point counts!

  • Win = 3 points
  • Loss = 1 point
  • Individual and team points are combined.
  • The team with the highest total points wins!
